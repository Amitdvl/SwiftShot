# Concept exploration

Built-in image-generation prompt used before vector refinement:

Design an original premium app brand symbol for SwiftShot, a fast Mac screenshot tool. Output ONE symbol only on actual transparent background, 1024x1024. NOT an app tile, not a mockup. The concept is 'a captured instant': two substantial, elegantly curved ivory-white shutter ribbons sweep past each other in 180-degree rotational balance, forming an abstract S-like silhouette and framing a small precise square aperture of empty negative space at the center. The two forms should feel fast, decisive and optically balanced: broad calm outer arcs transitioning into crisp diagonal inner cuts. Think confident contemporary identity design at the standard of leading independent Mac apps. Extreme economy: only TWO solid filled shapes, generous negative space, no outlines, no tiny details. Sculpt the contour beautifully, ensure it is ownable and not a generic S font, not a yin-yang, not chain links, not the Swift bird logo, not the Loom logo, not a pinwheel, no literal camera, no cursor, no overlapping windows, no viewfinder corner brackets, no lightning bolt. Strict flat ivory-white shapes only; NO gradients, shadows, bevels, highlights, texture or 3D. Icon Composer will provide the material finish later. Mark should occupy about 65% of square canvas, centered. Make this a striking memorable original symbol, with the small square of captured space as the clever visual idea.
